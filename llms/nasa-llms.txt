# NASA Open APIs

> NASA (National Aeronautics and Space Administration) provides a suite of free public APIs at api.nasa.gov offering access to space, Earth science, and aeronautics data. All APIs authenticate with a single `api_key` query parameter (use `DEMO_KEY` to explore). Signed-up keys allow 1,000 requests/hour; DEMO_KEY is limited to 30/hour and 50/day per IP.

## APIs

- [Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD)](https://api.nasa.gov/#apod): The daily astronomy image or video with an expert explanation.
- [Mars Rover Photos](https://api.nasa.gov/#MarsPhotos): Images from the Curiosity, Opportunity, and Spirit rovers, by sol/date and camera.
- [NeoWs (Near Earth Object Web Service)](https://api.nasa.gov/#NeoWS): Near-Earth asteroid data from the NASA JPL Asteroid team.
- [DONKI (Space Weather)](https://api.nasa.gov/#DONKI): Space weather events and notifications (CME, solar flares, geomagnetic storms, and more).
- [EPIC (Earth Polychromatic Imaging Camera)](https://api.nasa.gov/#EPIC): Full-disk Earth imagery from the DSCOVR spacecraft.
- [Image and Video Library](https://api.nasa.gov/#Images): NASA's media archive of images, video, and audio (images-api.nasa.gov).
